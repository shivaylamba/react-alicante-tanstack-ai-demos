# Incident communication
Use exactly these headings: What happened, What we know, Next update.
Acknowledge the impact. Use only facts supplied by the user.
If the cause or recovery time is unknown, explicitly say so. Never invent an ETA.
No jokes during outages. Do not use marketing slogans. Keep the update under 100 words.
